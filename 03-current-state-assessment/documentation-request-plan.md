# Verdant Pay Limited
## Stage 3, Part 1: Documentation Request Plan
**Confidential | Internal Use Only**

## Purpose

This document outlines the structured documentation request plan for 
the Verdant Pay cyber risk assessment. It identifies the specific 
evidence required from each of the seven stakeholders across the four 
defined scope areas. Every request is justified by either a scope area 
requirement, a flagged incident from Q1 2026, or a regulatory obligation 
under the CBN Cybersecurity Framework, NDPA 2023, or PCI-DSS.

The plan deliberately distinguishes between two categories of evidence: 
policy documents that show what should exist, and technical evidence 
that shows what actually exists. That distinction is critical because 
the gap between the two is where risk lives.

## Documentation Request by Stakeholder

### 1. Head of IT Infrastructure

Current user provisioning process documentation showing how new staff 
accounts are created and what access is assigned at onboarding. 
Offboarding process documentation showing the steps IT follows when 
a staff member exits. Records of how IT receives and acts on offboarding 
notifications from People Operations. Privileged access assignments and 
review history showing who holds elevated access and when that was last 
reviewed. Conditional access policy configuration documentation. 
Technical access review records showing periodic confirmation that access 
assignments remain appropriate and that no unauthorized or outdated 
access exists.

---

### 2. Cloud Administrator

Documentation of how customer data is stored in Azure and which specific 
systems hold it. Current permission reports showing who has access to 
SharePoint folders containing customer financial data. Configuration 
exports showing the actual technical state of the Azure and M365 
environment right now, not what policy says should be configured but 
what is actually in place. Records of when permissions were last reviewed 
and by whom. Any identified misconfigurations or unintended access points. 
Evidence showing whether current technical configurations match what the 
DPO's data handling policies say should be in place.

---

### 3. Head of People Operations

Formal documentation of the onboarding process covering how new staff 
access requirements are communicated to IT. Formal offboarding 
notification process documentation showing how and when IT is notified 
to deactivate access when staff exit. Cybersecurity training completion 
records for all staff. Training frequency schedule and any knowledge 
assessment results showing actual retention beyond completion. Records 
of staff acknowledgment and sign-off on key policies including the 
acceptable use policy. Documentation of how sensitive internal HR data 
including employment contracts, salary records, and performance 
information is stored and handled internally.

---

### 4. Data Protection Officer

Data classification policy showing how customer personal and financial 
data is formally categorized. Acceptable use policy covering how customer 
data may be used across all internal stakeholders. Consent mechanism 
documentation showing how customer consent is obtained and recorded. 
Breach notification history and any regulatory correspondence related 
to customer data protection. Compliance audit trails providing evidence 
of how the organization has met its NDPA 2023 obligations. Data subject 
request handling procedures. Current data flow documentation showing how 
customer data moves through the organization day to day.

---

### 5. Chief Compliance Officer

Current status of all key policies including acceptable use, data 
classification, incident response, and third-party risk management, 
including date of last review for each. Findings from the most recent 
CBN examination including a full record of what was identified. Status 
of any open remediation commitments from that examination showing what 
has been addressed and what remains outstanding. Current internal 
assessment of examination readiness for Q4 2026. Any regulatory 
correspondence received from CBN since the last examination. Internal 
and external compliance frameworks currently in operation at Verdant Pay.

---

### 6. Chief Information Security Officer

Security controls currently in place across all four scope areas 
including how each control is reviewed and by whom. Documentation of 
what controls were specifically designed or implemented in response to 
the three Q1 2026 flagged incidents and evidence that those controls 
are now operating effectively. Register of known security gaps currently 
being tracked and their remediation status. The organization's formally 
defined risk tolerance or risk appetite documentation. Incident response 
plan and evidence of how it operates in practice beyond the written 
document. Overall security posture documentation covering identity and 
access management, customer data handling, cybersecurity awareness, and 
governance posture.

---

### 7. Senior Product Operations Manager

Any informal process guides or team SOPs the operations team uses 
internally that may not be formally documented or known to IT or 
compliance. Any third-party tools or platforms the operations team uses 
that may not be formally approved by IT or reviewed for security. Any 
internal communications, trackers, or shared files that involve customer 
data outside of officially sanctioned systems. Information about how the 
operations team actually behaves around security practices under pressure, 
including whether staff follow data handling guidelines when deadlines 
are tight, whether credentials are shared informally, and whether 
unapproved workarounds are used when official processes are slow or 
inconvenient.

## Summary of Evidence Categories

Every documentation request across all seven stakeholders falls into 
one of three justification categories:

**Scope area requirement:** The document is needed to assess one of 
the four defined areas.

**Flagged incident:** The document is needed to understand the cause, 
impact, or current remediation status of one of the three Q1 2026 
concerns.

**Regulatory obligation:** The document is needed to assess compliance 
with CBN Cybersecurity Framework, NDPA 2023, or PCI-DSS requirements.

No documentation request exists outside these three justifications. 
Every piece of evidence requested has a clear purpose tied directly 
to the assessment objective.
