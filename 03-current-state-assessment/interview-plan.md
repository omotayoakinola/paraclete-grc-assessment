# Verdant Pay Limited
## Stage 3, Part 2: Stakeholder Interview Plan
**Confidential | Internal Use Only**

## Purpose

This document outlines the structured stakeholder interview plan for 
the Verdant Pay cyber risk assessment. While Part 1 identified the 
documentation required from each stakeholder, this plan addresses 
what documentation alone cannot answer: the actual operational reality 
of how processes work at Verdant Pay, where handoffs break down, how 
quickly incidents are responded to, and how confident key personnel 
are in the organization's current security and compliance posture.

Every interview question in this plan is designed to surface one of 
four things:

- Process gaps between documented procedure and actual practice
- Timing realities around critical security processes
- Personal assessments of control effectiveness and regulatory readiness
- Honest identification of known vulnerabilities that no policy 
  document will formally acknowledge

## Interview Approach

Each stakeholder will be engaged through a structured one-on-one 
interview conducted by the GRC team. Interviews will be tailored 
specifically to each role rather than using a generic question list, 
ensuring that each session extracts information only that stakeholder 
can provide.

Every interview will be preceded by a short written communication 
explaining the purpose of the session, what areas will be covered, 
and any documentation the stakeholder should have available. Following 
each interview, a summary of findings will be circulated back to the 
relevant stakeholder for validation before findings are incorporated 
into the current state assessment.

Where an interview response references a document not already requested 
in Part 1, a formal documentation request will be raised immediately 
following that session.

## Interview Questions by Stakeholder

### 1. Head of IT Infrastructure

This interview focuses on the technical reality of identity and access 
management at Verdant Pay, specifically around the provisioning and 
deprovisioning of staff access and the communication process between 
IT and People Operations.

- When an employee leaves Verdant Pay, at what point do you revoke 
  their privileged access and how quickly does that happen?
- When a new employee is onboarded, how many days or hours does it 
  take to grant them the access they need to do their job?
- Do you communicate back to HR after onboarding or offboarding an 
  employee, and if yes, what is the timing of that communication?
- Do you record that confirmation immediately and where is that 
  record held?

---

### 2. Cloud Administrator

This interview focuses on the technical reality of cloud environment 
management, permission structures, and incident response capability 
within the Azure and M365 environment.

- When and how were access permissions last reviewed and by whom?
- When there is an incident related to the cloud environment, what 
  does the Cloud Administrator or team do about it and how quickly 
  is a response initiated?
- The compliance review in Q1 2026 identified that SharePoint 
  permissions had expanded beyond originally intended boundaries. 
  When did you first become aware of this and what was your response 
  in the first three hours after discovery?

---

### 3. Head of People Operations

This interview focuses on the employee lifecycle process as it connects 
to identity and access management, cybersecurity awareness, and 
governance posture. People Operations sits at the critical handoff 
point between HR processes and IT access control, which is directly 
relevant to one of the three flagged incidents.

- When an employee is onboarded or offboarded, how many hours does 
  it take HR to notify IT about the required change in access 
  permissions?
- How does HR confirm that IT has actually acted on that notification?
- How do you prove that all employees demonstrate actual knowledge 
  retention beyond simply completing security training?

---

### 4. Data Protection Officer

This interview focuses on the practical application of data protection 
obligations under NDPA 2023, how customer data classification works 
in reality, and how the organization responded to the absence of a 
formal data classification policy identified in Q1 2026.

- How and when is data classification applied across the organization 
  and who is responsible for enforcing it?
- How do you know and prove that all employees are following the 
  acceptable use guidelines in practice rather than just on paper?
- How do you respond when a customer data breach or potential breach 
  is identified, and what does that process look like in practice?
- How does customer data move through the organization day to day 
  and who has visibility over that flow?
- When the compliance team flagged the absence of a data 
  classification policy in Q1 2026, what was your assessment of 
  the potential regulatory exposure that created under NDPA 2023?

---

### 5. Chief Compliance Officer

This interview focuses on the organization's regulatory standing with 
CBN, the current state of key policies, and honest assessment of 
examination readiness ahead of the Q4 2026 CBN examination. The CCO 
holds intelligence about open findings and unresolved gaps that no 
formal document will fully capture.

- How confident are you personally that Verdant Pay's current 
  policies would satisfy a CBN examiner who walked in tomorrow, 
  and which policy area concerns you most?
- Are there any findings from the last CBN examination that have 
  not been fully closed yet?
- If you could fix one governance gap before Q4 2026, what would 
  it be?

---

### 6. Chief Information Security Officer

This interview focuses on the CISO's honest assessment of control 
effectiveness across all four scope areas and their personal view 
of where the organization's security posture is most exposed. The 
CISO holds strategic visibility into known gaps and risk tolerance 
decisions that formal security documentation will not fully reflect.

- Of the controls currently in place across all four scope areas, 
  which ones are you least confident are operating effectively?
- If a CBN examiner walked in tomorrow, which area of your security 
  posture would you be most concerned about?

---

### 7. Senior Product Operations Manager

This interview focuses on ground-level operational reality, 
specifically how the operations team actually uses systems and 
handles customer data in practice. This stakeholder is the primary 
source of information about workarounds, informal processes, and 
shadow IT that no technical scan or policy review will surface.

- What internal communications, trackers, or shared files does 
  your team use that involve customer data outside of officially 
  sanctioned systems?
- How does the operations team actually behave around security 
  practices when under pressure, including whether staff follow 
  data handling guidelines when deadlines are tight?
- What third-party tools or platforms does the operations team 
  use that may not be formally approved by IT or reviewed for 
  security?

## Interview Finding Documentation

Following each interview, findings will be recorded in a structured 
interview log capturing:

- Stakeholder name and role
- Date of interview
- Key findings per question
- Any documents referenced that require formal follow-up requests
- Any variances identified between what the stakeholder described 
  and what existing documentation states

Variances between documented procedure and actual practice will be 
flagged immediately as potential risk findings and carried forward 
into the current state assessment report at the conclusion of Stage 3.
